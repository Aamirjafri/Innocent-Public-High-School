Welcome to The Innocent Public High School

Founded in 1984, The Innocent Public High School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Latifabad, Hyderabad. We believe every child deserves an environment that encourages curiosity, discipline, and holistic growth.

Our mission is to inspire young minds through academic excellence, moral values, and a supportive community that prepares students for the challenges of tomorrow.

  • Strong focus on academic excellence and discipline
  • Experienced and dedicated teaching staff
  • Safe and nurturing learning environment
  • Emphasis on character building and values
  • Located in the heart of Latifabad, Hyderabad

ACADEMICS

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive education that builds a strong foundation for higher studies and life.

Primary Section

Building strong fundamentals in a caring and engaging environment for young learners.

  • Core subjects: English, Mathematics, Science, Urdu
  • Focus on reading, writing & numeracy
  • Interactive and activity-based learning
  • Moral education & good habits

Middle Section

Developing deeper understanding and independent learning skills.

  • Expanded curriculum with social studies
  • Introduction to computer literacy
  • Project work & group activities
  • Emphasis on discipline and responsibility

High School Section

Preparing students for board examinations and future academic success.

  • Secondary & Higher Secondary preparation
  • Science, Commerce & general streams
  • Exam-focused guidance & practice
  • Career counseling support
